Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

FourSight is a personal app used by a single person, its developer, to view analytics for their own social media accounts. This policy explains what data FourSight handles and what it does with it.

Who uses FourSight

Only the developer. FourSight isn't offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data FourSight accesses

With the account owner's permission, given through each platform's official sign-in, FourSight reads:

  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID), account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts) and the owner's public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • YouTube: the owner's channel, videos and YouTube Analytics data, such as views, watch time, retention and subscriber changes.
  • Facebook: insights for a Facebook Page the owner manages, including Reels statistics.
  • Instagram: insights for the owner's Instagram professional account and its media.
  • Google Drive: a single FourSight folder in the owner's Google Drive, used to share FourSight's data between the owner's own devices. FourSight can only see files it created there, not the rest of the owner's Drive.

If the owner chooses to post a video, FourSight uploads that video to the owner's own account on the selected platform, only after the owner confirms the post.

How the data is stored

  • Analytics are stored in a database on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only in the operating system's secure storage (Windows Credential Manager or the Android Keystore). They are never written to Google Drive, to exports or to logs.
  • FourSight has no server. No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Sharing

FourSight does not sell, rent, share or disclose any data to third parties, and it isn't used for advertising.

Platform terms and policies

FourSight uses YouTube API Services. By using FourSight's YouTube features, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. FourSight's access to TikTok, Facebook and Instagram data is also governed by those platforms' own terms and privacy policies.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings: TikTok's "Manage app permissions," Google account permissions, and Facebook's and Instagram's "Apps and websites" settings.
  • Disconnecting an account in FourSight deletes its stored tokens from the device.
  • Deleting FourSight's data on the devices and its folder in Google Drive removes all stored analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
